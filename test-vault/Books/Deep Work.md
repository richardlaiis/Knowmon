# Deep Work

The argument: the ability to focus without distraction on cognitively demanding work is becoming rarer and more valuable at the same time, so it's worth training deliberately.

## Key points

- **Deep vs. shallow work**: deep work creates new value and is hard to replicate; shallow work (email, meetings, logistics) is necessary but easy to replicate.
- **Attention residue**: switching tasks leaves part of your attention on the previous one, so constant checking costs more than it seems.
- **Schedule depth**: pick a rhythm (fixed daily blocks worked for me) instead of waiting for inspiration.
- **Embrace boredom**: if you reach for your phone at every pause, focus is hard to switch on when you need it.
- **Shutdown ritual**: end the workday explicitly so unfinished tasks stop nagging in the evening.

## How it went for me

Morning blocks stuck, afternoon blocks didn't ([[2026-10-06]], [[2026-10-15]]). Pairing the block with a habit cue, as in [[Atomic Habits]], is what made it last.
