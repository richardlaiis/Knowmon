# Thinking in Systems

An introduction to seeing the world as stocks, flows and feedback loops.

## Vocabulary

- **Stock**: something that accumulates (water in a bathtub, fitness, unread email).
- **Flow**: what fills or drains a stock.
- **Balancing loop**: pushes a stock towards a goal (a thermostat).
- **Reinforcing loop**: amplifies change in either direction (compound interest, burnout).
- **Delay**: the time between a change and its effect, the main reason systems overshoot.

## Where I noticed it

- Training load is a stock; fatigue responds with a delay, so ramping too fast feels fine until it suddenly isn't ([[2026-10-28]]).
- My afternoon focus problem from [[Deep Work]] looks like a balancing loop: the more I push, the more tired I get.
- Habits from [[Atomic Habits]] are reinforcing loops when they work.
