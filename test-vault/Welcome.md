# Welcome to Knowmon

This is the sample vault. It's a small but realistic knowledge base: about seven weeks of one person's journal, reading notes, two side projects and a few recipes. Open the graph (`Ctrl+G`) or the timeline (`Ctrl+Shift+T`) to see how the notes connect.

## Where things are

- **Journal**: daily entries such as [[2026-10-05]] and [[2026-11-23]]. Their file names are dates, so they form a chain in the graph and line up on the timeline.
- **Books**: [[Atomic Habits]], [[Deep Work]], [[The Pragmatic Programmer]] and [[Thinking in Systems]].
- **Projects**
  - Knowmon itself: [[Dev log]], [[Design notes]], [[Time context links]] and [[Knowmon/Ideas|a list of ideas]].
  - Running: the [[Training plan]] for a half marathon and [[Half marathon/Ideas|race-day ideas]].
- **Meetings**: [[2026-10-14 Design review]] and [[2026-11-09 Weekly planning]].
- **Cooking**: [[Tomato egg stir-fry]], [[Miso soup]] and [[Weeknight pasta]].
- **Reference**: [[Markdown showcase]] shows everything the editor can render.
- **中文**: a few notes in Chinese, for trying Chinese search and summaries. Start with [[中文筆記說明]].
