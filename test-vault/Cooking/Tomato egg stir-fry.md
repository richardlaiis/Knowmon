# Tomato egg stir-fry

Ten minutes, one pan. Serves two with rice.

## Ingredients

- 4 eggs
- 3 ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 spring onion, sliced
- 1 tsp sugar, salt to taste
- 2 tbsp oil

## Steps

1. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt. Scramble them in hot oil until just set, then take them out.
2. Fry the tomatoes until they release their juice and start to break down, about 3 minutes.
3. Add the sugar and salt, return the eggs, stir once and turn off the heat.
4. Top with the spring onion.

Tip: softer tomatoes make a better sauce. Add a splash of water if they're firm.
