# Weeknight pasta

Garlic, chili and olive oil, with whatever greens are around.

| Ingredient   |  For one |  For two |
| ------------ | -------: | -------: |
| Spaghetti    |    100 g |    200 g |
| Garlic       | 2 cloves | 4 cloves |
| Chili flakes |  a pinch |    ½ tsp |
| Olive oil    |   2 tbsp |   4 tbsp |
| Spinach      |     50 g |    100 g |

1. Cook the pasta in well-salted water; keep a cup of the cooking water.
2. Warm the oil with sliced garlic and chili over low heat until the garlic is golden.
3. Toss in the pasta, spinach and a splash of pasta water until glossy.
