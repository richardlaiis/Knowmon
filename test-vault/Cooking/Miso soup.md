# Miso soup

## Ingredients

- 800 ml dashi (or water with a piece of kombu, soaked for 30 minutes)
- 2–3 tbsp miso paste (started with 4, much too salty)
- 150 g silken tofu, cubed
- A handful of wakame
- Spring onion

## Steps

1. Heat the dashi until it's steaming, but don't let it boil.
2. Add the tofu and wakame and warm them through.
3. Turn off the heat. Dissolve the miso in a ladle of the broth, then stir it in. Boiling miso dulls its flavor.
4. Serve with spring onion.

First attempt on [[2026-11-16]]: too salty. Use half the miso next time.
