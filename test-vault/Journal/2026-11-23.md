# 2026-11-23

Race day. Finished the half marathon in 1:56:12, an average pace of about 5:30 /km. The plan was 5:25, and I held it until kilometre 16, then the calf complained and I eased off. Still a personal best by eleven minutes.

What worked, from the [[Half marathon/Ideas|race-day checklist]]: eating breakfast three hours before, starting slower than felt necessary, and a gel every 5 km. Seven weeks of the [[Training plan]], done.
