# 2026-10-28

Reading [[Thinking in Systems]]. The chapter on feedback loops made me look at training differently: fatigue is a balancing loop with a delay, which is exactly why ramping mileage too fast feels fine for a week and then suddenly doesn't.

Cut this week's long run from 18 km to 16 km after a twinge in my left calf. Better to arrive at the start line slightly undertrained than injured.
