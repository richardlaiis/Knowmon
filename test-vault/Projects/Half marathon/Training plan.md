## Pace

Pace is time divided by distance, $p = t / d$. For a goal time $t$ over $d = 21.1$ km:

$$
p = \frac{1\,\text{h}\;54\,\text{min}}{21.1\,\text{km}} = \frac{6840\,\text{s}}{21.1\,\text{km}} \approx 324\,\text{s/km} \approx 5{:}24\ \text{/km}
$$

A rough race-time predictor from a shorter race ($t_1$ over $d_1$) is $t_2 = t_1 \left( d_2 / d_1 \right)^{1.06}$. From the 10K on [[2026-11-02]] (51:40) it predicts about 1:54.

## Heart rate zones

Easy runs stay in zone 2 (below 150 bpm). Long runs may drift into zone 3 in the last few kilometres.
